Privacy Policy

How Jb.rides collects, protects and uses your data.

1. Information We Collect

At Jb.rides, we collect personal information necessary to facilitate scooter and bike rental services in accordance with Indian Motor Vehicles regulations. This includes:

  • Government identification proofs (Driving License number and copy, Aadhar Card or Voter ID).

The collected details are strictly utilized to:
