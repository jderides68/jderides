Terms & Conditions

Please read our rental guidelines before hiring a bike or scooty from Jb.rides.

1. Rider Eligibility & Verification

  • The rider must be at least 18 years of age at the time of taking possession of the vehicle.
  • A valid Original Driving License (Two-Wheeler / Gear / Non-Gear as applicable) and original Aadhaar card or Voter ID must be presented for physical verification at our Tri Nagar shop.
  • Learner's License (LL) is not acceptable for renting self-drive motorcycles or scooters.

2. Security Deposit & Payments

  • A refundable security deposit ranging between ₹1,000 to ₹2,500 (depending on vehicle model: Activa / Pulsar / Royal Enfield) is required before handover.
  • Deposits can be paid via UPI, Cash, or Net Banking.
  • The deposit is refunded instantly upon returning the vehicle in good working condition without major damage or traffic challans.

3. Fuel Policy

  • Vehicles are handed over with a minimal amount of fuel (usually enough to reach the nearest filling station).
  • The customer must return the vehicle with approximately the same fuel level as recorded during pickup. Unused petrol is non-refundable.

4. Traffic Laws & Safety

  • Wearing a helmet is mandatory for the rider and pillion passenger under Delhi Traffic Police regulations. One helmet is provided complimentary by Jb.rides.
  • Any traffic violations, e-challans, over-speeding fines, or parking penalties incurred during the rental tenure are the sole liability of the customer.
  • Riding under the influence of alcohol, narcotics, or dangerous stunt driving is strictly prohibited.

5. Vehicle Damage & Breakdown

  • Before driving off, the customer is invited to thoroughly inspect the vehicle, take photos/videos, and note existing scratches or dents on the rental handover slip.
  • Any physical damage caused to the bike/scooty during the rental period will be evaluated and charged as per authorized service center repair costs.
  • In the rare event of mechanical malfunction or puncture, contact our 24x7 support team immediately for prompt assistance.

6. Late Return & Rental Extension

  • If you wish to extend your rental duration, please notify us at least 3 hours prior to your scheduled return time via WhatsApp or call.
  • Unauthorized delay in vehicle return beyond a 30-minute grace period will incur standard hourly or full-day late return penalty charges.
