Guidelines and rules for renting White Electric Scooties from jdrides.

1. Required Documents & Age Eligibility

  • The rider must be at least 18 years of age at the time of taking handover of the E-Scooty.
  • Mandatory Verification Documents: Original Valid Indian Driving License (Two-Wheeler) and Original Aadhaar Card must be shown physically at our Tri Nagar shop.
  • Learner's License (LL) is not acceptable for self-drive rental.

2. Security Deposit & Instant Refund

  • A nominal refundable security deposit of ₹1,000 is required before taking possession of the White E-Scooty.
  • Deposit can be submitted via UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or Cash.
  • The security deposit is refunded immediately upon returning the vehicle in good working order without unnotified physical damage.

3. EV Battery & Charging Guidelines

  • The vehicle is handed over with a full or near-full battery charge (providing approx. 100-120 KM range).
  • A genuine portable smart home charger is supplied with every rental at zero extra charge.
  • Always plug the charger into a properly earthed, standard 3-pin household 15A/5A socket. Avoid using cheap multi-plugs.
  • Do not expose the charging socket or adapter to direct heavy rain or water immersion.

4. Traffic Laws & Helmet Mandatory

  • Wearing a helmet is strictly mandatory for the rider and pillion passenger under Delhi Traffic Police regulations. One sanitized ISI helmet is provided complimentary by jdrides.
  • Any traffic violations, e-challans, over-speeding fines, red light jumping, or unauthorized parking fines incurred during the rental period are the sole liability of the customer.
  • Riding under the influence of alcohol, narcotics, or dangerous stunt driving is strictly prohibited.

5. Vehicle Damage & Maintenance

  • Customer is requested to inspect the E-Scooty (body panels, lights, brakes, digital meter) and take photos before driving off.
  • Any physical damage caused to the vehicle during rental will be charged as per standard authorized repair replacement prices.
  • In the rare event of puncture or technical issue, contact our 24x7 support number immediately: +91 95821 16104.
